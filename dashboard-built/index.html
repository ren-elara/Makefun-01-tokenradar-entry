READ THE EVIDENCE

What these signals describe

Observed

TVL, seven-day TVL change, tracking date, token record, category and chain labels at one frozen date.

Unobserved

Launch intent, allocation, fees, holders, wallet eligibility, tokenomics, security audits and historical market cap.

Selection effect

Comparables are surviving traded tokens. Their valuation dispersion cannot establish launch odds for another protocol.

Missing values

Missing dates display unknown and use 365 days for seasoning. Missing change uses neutral growth and Unknown momentum.
