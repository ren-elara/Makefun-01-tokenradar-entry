WHAT THE SNAPSHOT CAN AND CANNOT SAY

Evidence before inference

Observed

TVL, seven-day TVL change, DefiLlama tracking date, market-cap record, category and chain labels at one frozen date.

Not observed

Fees, revenue, holders, wallets, tokenomics, allocation, user growth, launch intent, exploit history or historical market cap.

Selection effect

Market-cap comparables are survivors with a token record. Their distribution is not a launch probability for protocols without one.

Missing values

Missing tracking dates display unknown and use 365d for seasoning. Null seven-day change uses a neutral growth adjustment.
